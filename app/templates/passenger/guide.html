Passenger Guide

Boarding Procedure

1 Register Online or Walk-In
2 Wait in the Queue
3 Verify Your Registration
4 Listen for Boarding Calls
5 Board the Boat
6 Travel to Your Destination

Where to go

Proceed to the terminal registration area upon arrival.

What to prepare

Bring a valid ID and show your Reference Code to verify.

Check-in process

Arrive 30 minutes early, and wait for the boarding announcement.

Luggage & Baggage Rules

Allowed
  • Hand-carry bags up to 7kg are allowed onboard
  • Checked baggage up to 20kg per passenger
Not Allowed / Restrictions
  • Flammable materials, sharp objects, and hazardous items are prohibited
  • Oversized cargo requires prior arrangement

Frequently Asked Questions

Each trip has a designated passenger capacity. Registration will be unavailable once the trip reaches its maximum capacity.

Weather and lake conditions are monitored for passenger safety. Boarding decisions are subject to the authorized terminal and Coast Guard personnel.

Listen for the boarding announcement from the terminal operator and remain within the designated waiting area.

About the Terminal

A modern passenger terminal offering safe and efficient island routes through a digital manifest system. With environmental monitoring and safety-certified vessels, we ensure smooth operations, and secure travel.
